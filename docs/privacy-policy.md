# Privacy policy: Well-Being Companion

Last updated: [date]

Well-Being Companion ("the app") helps you track workouts, habit breaks, sleep and screen time. This policy says what it stores, where, and who can see it.

Contact: [your contact email]

## What stays on your phone

Everything you log (workouts, break reminders and counts, sleep, screen-time totals, settings) is stored on your phone first. The app works fully without an account. If you never sign in, none of it leaves your phone.

## If you sign in

Signing in with Google creates an account. We receive your name and email address from Google. Your entries and settings are then backed up to our server (hosted by Supabase) so you can use more than one phone. Only you can read your own entries: the server enforces this for every record.

## Groups

A group is made of people who joined it with an invite link. For each group you choose what you share:

- **Workouts:** the type and length of workouts you log, and your weekly totals, shown on the group's activity feed and leaderboard.
- **Break goals:** when you meet a break goal, and streak milestones, shown on the feed and counted towards your active days.

Group members also see your name. You can turn either switch off at any time and it hides your earlier items straight away. **Sleep and screen time are never shared with groups.** Leaving a group removes you from it.

## Screen time

If you allow Usage access in your phone's settings, the app reads how long apps were in the foreground. It keeps only daily totals, minutes per category and minutes per hour. App names are not stored or sent.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They do not use a server.

## What we do not do

We do not show ads, sell data, or use analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not use your data to train models.

## Your choices

- **Export** everything on your phone as a JSON or CSV file: Settings, Your data.
- **Erase** the data on your phone: Settings, Your data (when signed out).
- **Delete your account:** Settings, Delete account. This removes your account and everything stored for it on the server, including your place in groups. Data on your phone stays until you erase it.

## Not medical advice

The app's insights and tips are general wellness information, not medical advice.

## Children

The app is intended for adults (18 and over).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the app's release notes say so.
